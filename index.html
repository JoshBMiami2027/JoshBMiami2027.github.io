02 / Selected projects

Work in motion.

Two sports analytics projects built around prediction, similarity, and making results easier to interpret.

Shot prediction
Project 01 · Predictive modeling

ShotIQ

A pro athlete shot prediction model that turns shot probabilities into a more intuitive measure of difficulty and scoring efficiency.

Logistic regressionRandom forestVisualization
Explore the project
  • Engineered inputs such as shot distance, angle, and defender proximity.
  • Trained classification models to predict shot outcomes and converted probabilities into a shot difficulty score.
  • Compared expected and actual performance to identify efficient scorers.
  • Visualized shot zones and efficiency through heatmaps.
Player similarity
Project 02 · Similarity modeling

MyDNA

A sports analytics tool that matches a user’s performance profile to real NBA players through statistical similarity.

400+ player profilesCosine similarity<0.5s per query
Explore the project
  • Processed stat profiles for more than 400 NBA players.
  • Applied feature scaling and standardization to reduce bias from variables with different ranges.
  • Used cosine similarity to rank players across multiple statistical dimensions in under half a second per query.
  • Returned top matches above 90% similarity on test inputs.
